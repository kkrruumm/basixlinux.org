# IRC

We have a chatroom on [oftc](https://www.oftc.net/) at `#basixlinux`.

You can join here to (maybe) get some help with issues you're having, hang out, discuss packaging, the usual.

There aren't strict on-topic rules for this channel. Just don't be an asshole- come say hi, hang out, have fun.

## Why OFTC?

Because liberachat has shown themselves to be hostile to VPNs for, in my opinion, largely arbitrary reasons. I hold no faith that in the future users will still be able to connect with Tor even given how my support request in regards to this was just shrugged off.

# Forum

The closest thing to a forum Basix has (and will probably ever have) is a lemmy page [here](https://lemmy.ml/c/basixlinux).

# Mastodon

You can follow me [here](https://procursus.social/@krum) if you'd like, occasionally I may post updates and comments on Basix. For now, I see no need for a big fancy official account.
